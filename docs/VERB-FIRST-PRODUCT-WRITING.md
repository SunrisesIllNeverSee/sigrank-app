## Doctrine (verbatim)

**Posting hierarchy:**

1. Explain with verbs.
2. Give each post one primary reader behavior: STOP / READ / REPLY / QUOTE / SEND / FOLLOW.
3. AM is the anchor; PM should perform a different job.
4. Images prove; copy interprets.
5. Articles are mined for ideas and lines, not merely promoted.
6. Strongest research/material is concentrated Tuesday–Thursday.

**Verb-first test** — before publishing any product description:

- Lead with what happens or what the system does.
- Name the product only after the action is understandable.
- Introduce theory/terminology only after the reader has a concrete mental model.

**Preferred order:** `action → consequence → product name → theory`.

## Generalized rule (beyond publishing)

When writing or describing a product — README, landing copy, wiki, canon
definitions, guide openings — describe what it *does* before naming what it
*is*. Nouns label; verbs teach. The reader should understand the action before
they ever see the brand.
